❄️ How to Play

  • 1 Two teams take turns picking a numbered Frosted Ice Tile from the board.
  • 2 Answer the Multiple-Choice Question together as a team.
  • 3 Correct Answer = Basketball Shot! Time the moving power meter to sink a 2-Pointer or hit the green center zone for a 3-Point Swish!
  • 4 If the answer is wrong, no shot is awarded and the turn passes to the other team. Highest score when all tiles are cleared wins!
